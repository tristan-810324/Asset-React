# Master Prompt: A.SSET Logo System

**Written for:** a designer or an AI image generator that will create the A.SSET logo.

## Product architecture (what the logo represents)

- **Core job:** track school assets from procurement and receiving, through item tagging (barcodes and unit tags), issuance to employees or rooms, return, repair, loss and damage cases, and settlement.
- **Other modules:** service work orders (facilities and IT jobs), facility and equipment reservations, purchase requisitions, and requester identity verification.
- **Users:** requesters, GSO Staff, GSO Head, IT Staff, IT Admin.
- **Ideas to express visually (one at most):** asset tag or barcode, custody and handover, a checked-off record, a campus or building, a shield for trust and audit.

## Brand system

| Role | Color |
|------|-------|
| Primary, dark backgrounds | Deep navy `#09244a` |
| Secondary | Royal blue `#17377f`, `#2d5aa3` |
| Accent (highlight and IT avatar) | Gold `#d6b333` |
| Success, "approved" states | Emerald `#138548` |
| Neutrals | Slate `#334155`, `#f1f5f9` |

**Typeface feel:** Plus Jakarta Sans, geometric and bold.

## Design brief

1. Give **3 distinct concepts**. Each has a symbol plus a wordmark "A.SSET". Optional sub-line: "Asset Management System" or "GSO Command Center".
2. The symbol must work alone at **16 px** (browser tab), **32 px**, and **512 px**.
3. Provide three versions: **one-color** (navy only), **white-reversed** (for dark navy backgrounds), and **full-color**.
4. Keep it **flat, vector, and simple**: 2 to 3 colors at most, no gradients, no photos, no 3D effects, no tiny details that vanish at small sizes.
5. Leave clear space around the mark. It must sit next to the Dr. Yanga's Colleges logo without visual conflict.
6. **Avoid:** copying any existing university or government seal, using the DYCI seal or shield, crowns, real brand logos, cartoon mascots, stock-photo textures, or the letters "DYCI" as part of the mark.

## Deliverables

- The 3 concepts as flat vector art (SVG-style), each with its one-color and reversed version.
- A short rationale per concept (2 sentences each).
- A square (1:1) favicon crop of the symbol only.

## Negative prompt

```
photorealistic, 3D render, gradient mesh, neon glow, drop shadow, skeuomorphic, cartoon, mascot, emoji, stock photo, government seal, copied university crest, illegible small text, more than three colors, busy background
```
